# PubSub SDK

## Standardizing message contracts

**Microsoft**

The PubSub SDK standardized message schemas for Teams Management Infrastructure and gave partner teams a common, reliable contract for distributed messaging. It was designed to handle dead-letter queues, out-of-order delivery, replay and rollback flows, and high-volume backfills while keeping integration patterns consistent across services.

### Highlights

- Designed robust messaging contracts for distributed systems
- Supported dead-letter queues and recovery workflows
- Enabled replay, rollback, and backfill scenarios at scale
- Simplified adoption across partner teams and service boundaries
- Included CI/CD and testing practices to improve reliability

### Technologies

.NET, C#, Azure, distributed messaging, and CI/CD tooling.
